Guidelines

Before you write.

We accept
  • 100% original articles, not published anywhere else.
  • 1,000 to 2,500 words, with clear headings and short paragraphs.
  • Practical tips, case studies, step-by-step guides and real results.
  • Facts and statistics linked to trusted sources.
  • Up to 1 link to your site in the body, plus 1 in your author bio.
  • Your own screenshots or royalty-free images (optional).
We don't accept
  • Copied, spun or unedited AI-generated content.
  • Articles written only for backlinks, or thinly disguised ads.
  • Gambling, adult, crypto-promotion, or misleading "get rich quick" topics.
  • Content already published on another website or your own blog.
  • Posts with excessive links or affiliate links.

Questions

Common questions.

Do you charge for guest posts?

No. Submitting and publishing a guest post is free, as long as it meets our guidelines.

Will I get a backlink?

Yes. You can include one relevant link in the article body and one in your author bio.

Can I republish my article elsewhere?

Please don't. Once published here, the article should stay unique to AMR.Z DigitalMarket. You can share the link anywhere you like.

How long until my article goes live?

Usually within 1 to 2 weeks after we receive and approve the final draft.

Can I write in Urdu or Roman Urdu?

We currently publish in English. Simple, clear English is perfect. No need for fancy words.
