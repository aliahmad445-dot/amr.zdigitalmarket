Questions

How do I get started?

Message us on WhatsApp for a free audit. We look at your business, your goals and your current situation, then suggest a plan. There is zero commitment.

Which platform will you run my ads on?

Every plan includes one platform of your choice: Facebook + Instagram, Google Ads or TikTok Ads.

Are there any hidden fees?

No. Every plan is tailored to your business stage, with no hidden fees.

Can I pay weekly instead of monthly?

Each plan lists both a weekly and a monthly price. Tell us which one you prefer when you message us.

Why do I see prices in my own currency?

We show an approximate conversion based on your location. Billing is in Pakistani rupees (PKR) at the current rate.

Will I see how my ads are doing?

Yes. We share reports on what worked, what didn't and what to do next, so you always see where your budget goes. The reporting for each plan is listed in the pricing section.
